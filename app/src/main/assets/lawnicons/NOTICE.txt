# Lawnicons masks

The 77 PNG masks in this directory are rasterizations of the original SVG artwork
from LawnchairLauncher/lawnicons v2.18.0, commit
`74b428b747004046686cbe5e24218e958afe2eb9`:
https://github.com/LawnchairLauncher/lawnicons/tree/74b428b747004046686cbe5e24218e958afe2eb9/svgs

Copyright remains with the Lawnicons contributors. The upstream Apache-2.0
license is included as `LICENSE.txt`.

MonoIcon renders the vectors at 768×768. Asset names, component aliases, artwork
and the proportional layout are retained. The source distribution records the
SVG and PNG hashes in `raster-sources.json` alongside these assets.

MonoIcon's project licence and third-party notices are published at
https://github.com/Jerrey-liang/MonoIcon/blob/main/docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md
